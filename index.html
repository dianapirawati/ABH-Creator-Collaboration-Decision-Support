Creator Collaboration Program

Abdi Creator Hub (ABH)

ABH is a creator collaboration program developed for Buku Abdi. It provides a structured pathway for working with book-content creators while preserving creator authenticity and generating measurable campaign evidence.

Barter is the collaboration mechanism; the strategic value lies in building a repeatable, measurable learning loop.

ABH also functions as a decision-support pathway by transforming campaign performance and creator feedback into structured evidence, actionable insight, and recommendations for subsequent campaign decisions. The final decision remains human-led by the marketing team.
