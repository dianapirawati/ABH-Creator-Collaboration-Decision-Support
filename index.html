AbdiCreatorHub: Creator Intelligence & Decision Support

A data-driven creator collaboration program developed for Buku Abdi to structure creator participation, capture campaign evidence, generate actionable insight, and support more informed decisions for subsequent campaigns.

Created by: Diana Putri Irawati (255288010) · Master's Programme in Marketing, Innovation and Technology, Department of Business Administration, POLBAN
Supervised by: Rivan Sutrisno, SE., MA., PhD

About the outcome

Barter is the collaboration mechanism — the strategic value is the measurable learning loop.

Program + Decision-Support Capability

What ABH changes

ABH provides a structured pathway for working with book-content creators while preserving creator authenticity and generating measurable campaign evidence.

Decision Support: campaign performance and creator feedback are transformed into structured evidence, actionable insight and recommendations. The final campaign decision remains human-led by the marketing team.
